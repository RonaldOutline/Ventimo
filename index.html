About Ventimo

Precision Engineering
Meets Craftsmanship

Ventimo is a Finnish maritime construction company with over two decades of experience delivering complex, high-specification construction and outfitting projects on some of the world's most prestigious vessels.

From the iconic Oasis of the Seas to the next-generation Icon of the Seas, our teams have contributed to vessels that have redefined what is possible in maritime engineering. We operate across major European and global shipyards, providing construction management, outfitting, and specialist technical services to shipbuilders and vessel owners alike.

Quality First Every weld, every joint, every finish — held to the highest maritime standard.
On Schedule Shipyard timelines are critical — we deliver on time, every time.
Expert Crews Highly trained maritime construction specialists with global shipyard experience.
What We Do

Comprehensive Maritime
Construction Services

From concept to completion, we provide the full spectrum of maritime construction and outfitting expertise.

01

New Build Outfitting

Full interior and exterior outfitting of new vessels from steel hull to final finish, coordinated with shipyard schedules and classification society requirements.

02

Renovation & Refit

Comprehensive vessel renovation and refitting services, modernising existing ships to meet contemporary standards while maintaining operational continuity.

03

Systems Engineering

Mechanical, electrical, HVAC, and piping systems integration — designed, installed, and commissioned by our experienced engineering teams.

04

Interior Construction

High-specification interior fit-out including cabins, public spaces, restaurants, entertainment venues, and technical areas for cruise and passenger vessels.

05

Structural Steel Work

Specialist structural steel fabrication, welding, and assembly for vessels across all classes — from cargo ships to the most complex cruise ships afloat.

06

Project Management

End-to-end project management services ensuring seamless coordination between owners, shipyards, subcontractors, and classification bodies throughout the build.
