Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Ventimo Oy ("Ventimo", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website contact form, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Who We Are

Ventimo Oy is a Finnish maritime construction company registered in Finland. Our registered address is Helsinki, Finland. For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at info@ventimo.fi.

2. What Data We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Company or organisation name (optional)
  • Vessel or project name (optional)
  • Project type and message content

3. Why We Collect It

We collect this data solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss a potential business engagement with you. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you tick the consent checkbox and submit the form. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at info@ventimo.fi.

5. How We Store Your Data

Form submissions are processed via Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service. Formspree stores submission data on their servers in accordance with their own privacy policy. We also receive submissions by email. Data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry, and no longer than 3 years unless a contractual relationship requires otherwise.

6. Who We Share It With

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared only with Formspree as our form processor, and with internal staff who need it to respond to your enquiry.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@ventimo.fi.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
